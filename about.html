MY STORY

From taking things apart to studying how to build them better.

I'm Samin, a first-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Waterloo from Calgary. My interest in engineering started long before I had the vocabulary for it — building with LEGO, working on cars, experimenting with 3D prints, and constantly wondering why something worked the way it did.

That curiosity eventually grew into an interest in CAD, robotics, programming and product design. I enjoy the cycle of making something, finding the weak point, and improving it.

Outside of technical work, leadership and community have shaped how I approach projects. Through Muslim Youth Club and other volunteer experiences, I've learned how important communication, mentorship and teamwork are when you're building something with other people.
